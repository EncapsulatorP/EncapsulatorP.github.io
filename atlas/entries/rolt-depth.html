A depth sweet spot we can actually solve

The product of two Gaussian depth profiles has an explicit optimum, making a useful controllable model for competing depth preferences.

RŌLT / phi-kappa depth conversations · December 2025 onward

S_i(d) = exp(−(d−μ_i)²/(2σ_i²)),  ℓ(d)=S₁(d)S₂(d)
d* = (μ₁/σ₁² + μ₂/σ₂²)/(1/σ₁² + 1/σ₂²)

Why follow it

We can expose every assumption behind a depth optimum and test whether real measurements support the chosen profiles.

For σ₁, σ₂ > 0, differentiate log ℓ(d), set the derivative to zero, and use its negative second derivative. The optimum is the inverse-variance weighted mean shown above.

On an integer depth grid, compare the neighboring allowed integers; a bounded depth range can move the optimum to its boundary.

The source’s illustrative choices include phi-related centers. Those are model inputs; any broader optimality claim needs measured objective functions and separate validation.
