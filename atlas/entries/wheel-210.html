Forty-eight arithmetic lanes

The 210-wheel is a complete finite skeleton for primes larger than seven when all 48 admissible remainder classes are retained.

PTP-210 conversations · December 2025 onward

210 = 2·3·5·7
φ(210) = 210(1−1/2)(1−1/3)(1−1/5)(1−1/7) = 48
p > 7 prime ⇒ gcd(p mod 210, 210) = 1

Why follow it

It separates an exact sieve structure from the interesting experimental question of how to order or weight its lanes.

The four small primes 2, 3, 5 and 7 are handled separately. Membership in one of the 48 lanes is necessary for a larger prime, while many lane members remain composite.

A selector may prioritize a subset, but a complete wheel sieve must eventually inspect every admissible class. A finite sample need not place equal numbers of primes in every class.

The historical PTP selector formulas changed across versions. This atlas preserves the stable arithmetic construction and links the current source rather than merging incompatible formulas.
