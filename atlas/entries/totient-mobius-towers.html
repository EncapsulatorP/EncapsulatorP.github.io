Why follow it

It couples two familiar arithmetic functions in a precisely specified nonlinear experiment.

The raw list is n = (3,5,6,7,10,11,13,14,15,17). Starting at 3 avoids the leading base φ(2) = 1, which would collapse the outer value.

The normalized list is n = (2,3,5,6,7,10,11,13,14,15,17,19), with bases 1 + φ(n)/n. Every listed n is squarefree, so its Möbius sign is ±1.

The finite values were independently recomputed at high precision. Changing the first index, sign alignment or association changes the construction and must be recorded.
