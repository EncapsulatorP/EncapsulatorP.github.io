Remainder-filtered Euler-product resolvents

Restrict the prime support of an Euler product, then compare its unsigned and multiplicative-parity series in their common convergence domain.

Active construction recovered from 8–13 June 2026

Z_A(s) = ∏_{p ∉ A}(1−p^(−s))⁻¹
L_A(s) = ∏_{p ∉ A}(1+p^(−s))⁻¹
L_A(s) = Z_A(2s)/Z_A(s),  Re s > 1

Why follow it

The construction has a precise algebraic core and a natural place for remainder filters, valuation kernels and parity comparisons.

A is a chosen set of excluded primes. Z_A sums over positive integers whose prime factors avoid A. L_A weights those integers by (−1)^Ω(n), the Liouville sign, including repeated prime factors.

The identity follows factor by factor from 1/(1+x) = (1−x)/(1−x²). Absolute convergence for Re s > 1 justifies the infinite products and their quotient.

“Resolvent” is the project’s construction name here. No spectral resolvent operator or continuation beyond the stated domain is supplied by this product identity alone.

Next experiment or question

Choose the remainder filter explicitly and compare its finite truncations before posing an analytic-continuation or correlation claim.
