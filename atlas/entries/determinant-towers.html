An inverse Collatz tree, viewed through operators

Inverse accelerated branches suggest a family of weighted composition operators organized by finite remainder automata.

Inverse-branch and determinant-tower conversations · March–May 2026

U_k⁻¹(m) = (2^k m − 1)/3
Proposed hierarchy: D_(2^a 3^b)(s)

Why follow it

It gives a potentially rich bridge between arithmetic branch admissibility and operator-theoretic cycle information.

An inverse branch is allowed only when it produces a positive odd integer with the intended exact valuation. The exponent k is an arithmetic condition, not a free continuous parameter.

The recovered proposal organizes admissible branches modulo 2^a3^b and seeks weighted composition operators, nuclearity, trace expansions and compatible Fredholm determinants.

The function space, weights and compatibility maps remain part of the proof task. A finite automaton or formal determinant notation does not establish the behavior of all integer orbits.

Next experiment or question

Start with one finite modulus and a specified function space; prove the operator and trace identities before taking a hierarchy limit.
