Zetheon: from forty-eight lanes to analytic channels

Prime remainder classes can index analytic sums and products, then be mixed with explicitly chosen weights.

PTP conversations · 10 December 2025 and 1 April 2026

Z_r(s) = Σ_{p ≡ r (mod 210)} p^(−s)
Π_r(s) = ∏_{p ≡ r (mod 210)}(1−p^(−s))⁻¹
Z_Θ(s) = Σ_r Θ(r) Z_r(s),  Re s > 1

Why follow it

This is a defined route from the finite wheel to complex-valued arithmetic observables.

Use the 48 coprime classes r modulo 210 and handle primes 2, 3, 5 and 7 separately. In Re s > 1, all displayed prime sums and Euler products converge absolutely.

The recovered conversation proposed entropy-like and character-weighted comparisons. The weights Θ and normalizations must be fixed before comparing constants or peaks.

Character decomposition provides established background through Dirichlet L-functions. A weighted numerical value is not automatically a new transcendental constant or a new theorem about prime distribution.
