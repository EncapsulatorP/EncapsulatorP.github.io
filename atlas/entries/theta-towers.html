Theta bases with alternating and golden-density signs

Jacobi theta values approaching one make a delicate finite tower whose sign schedule can be alternated or selected by a Beatty sequence.

Recovered June boundary notebook; finite truncations recomputed this edition

a_n = θ₃(0, exp(−πn)),  t_N = a_N
t_k = a_k^(s_(k+1) t_(k+1))
N = 10: 0.920156883755… or 1.086099670336…

Why follow it

The construction lets us compare deterministic sign rules while holding the bases and association order fixed.

Use s_k = (−1)^(k+1), with the first exponent sign s₂ negative, to get approximately 0.920156883755197601. Reverse the schedule to get approximately 1.086099670335550238.

For the golden rule, s_k is positive when k = floor(mφ) for a positive integer m, and negative otherwise. At depth 10 it gives approximately 0.920156791623985231.

These are right-associated finite towers with the exact convention t_N = a_N. Their close values invite a tail-sensitivity analysis; finite agreement is not an infinite-depth convergence result.

Next experiment or question

Increase depth with the terminal convention fixed and bound the influence of the tail as the theta bases approach one.
