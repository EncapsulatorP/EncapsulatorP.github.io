Collatz supplies a seed; a permutation supplies the guarantee

An affine modular schedule assigns distinct slots to distinct identifiers under an explicit coprimality condition.

Backoff engineering conversation · 21 February 2026

offset_k(id) = (a_k id + b_k) mod M
gcd(a_k,M)=1 ⇒ a permutation of the M slots

Why follow it

The engineering guarantee is finite and provable even when the coefficient generator comes from an exploratory dynamical sequence.

If two outputs agree modulo M, then a_k(id₁−id₂) is divisible by M. An inverse for a_k forces the identifiers to agree modulo M.

For M a power of two, choosing odd a_k is sufficient. Distinct identifiers modulo M receive distinct slots at the same scheduling step.

Requests spanning several slots or clients using different step origins require additional analysis. Collatz convergence is not an assumption of the permutation argument.

Next experiment or question

Test timing drift and request duration separately from the exact single-step slot assignment.
