The speed of light, through different written forms

A physical constant supplies a fixed input for studying which digit-factor patterns survive changes of units and representation.

Original May 2026 thread and Prime Forms continuation

299792458 → digit factor counts 122121213
Capping each count at two gives 122121212
The final 8 = 2³ carries one excess factor

Why follow it

It preserves the initiating question while making the representation choices visible and interactive.

The sequence shown counts prime factors with multiplicity in each decimal digit of the metre-per-second numerical value. Changing units changes that written numeral while the physical speed remains the same.

A digit’s largest prime factor and total factor count reconstruct prime powers, but do not reconstruct every composite. Mixed-factor digits need the full factor vector. Zero needs its own typed handling rather than a prime factorization.

This is a positional construction and a comparison of encodings. Any proposed physical implication needs a representation-independent quantity and a separate physical model.

Next experiment or question

Record unit, radix, digit convention and factor truncation together, then identify what remains unchanged under a declared transformation.
