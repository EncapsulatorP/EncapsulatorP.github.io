Listen to the carries in a digit packet

A finite base-p packet can be described by its valuation, remaining unit and depth of return toward a p-adic reference.

Boundary-path and p-adic packet experiments · recovered 2026 source

W(n,L) = Σ_{j=0}^{L−1} d_(n+j) p^j
r = v_p(W),  U = W/p^r
For odd p: Δ = v_p(U^(p−1) − 1)

Why follow it

It makes carry depth and multiplicative unit behavior visible in prime-gap or other arithmetic digit streams.

Digits are least-significant first. W must be nonzero before its finite valuation r and unit U are formed. For odd p, U mod p gives a unit-class label; it is not the full p-adic Teichmüller lift.

The implementation also uses the p = 2 branches U−1 and U+1, according to the unit class modulo 4. A zero difference has infinite valuation mathematically; the source’s display cap must be recorded as a cap.

These descriptors depend on the chosen base, packet length and input stream. Their use in separating arithmetic families is an experiment.
