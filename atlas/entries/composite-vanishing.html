A filter that removes composites needs a surviving pair

Finite small-prime filters can be convolved across a Goldbach sum. The hard target is a uniform positive lower bound after composite leakage is controlled.

Goldbach filter discussion · 11 June 2026

R_k(N) = Σ_m V_k(m)V_k(N−m)
V_k removes multiples of a chosen finite set of primes

Why follow it

It isolates the precise point where a useful sieve experiment would need a new argument.

Specify the support and the treatment of the filtering primes themselves. A product of divisibility indicators creates an exact finite candidate filter.

Surviving numbers can still be composite. Even if R_k(N) is positive, the surviving pair must be shown to be prime; increasing the filter changes the quantity under study.

The recovered thread proposed studying this leakage and the needed lower bound. No uniform Goldbach bound is established by the filter definition.

Next experiment or question

Measure survivor pairs and genuine prime pairs separately, then state a quantitative leakage bound that would be sufficient.
