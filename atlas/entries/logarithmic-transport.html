Carry a perturbation back through a power tower

A logarithm transfers an output perturbation back through one exponent layer exactly, before any linear approximation is made.

c3.2z analytic framework · recovered tower work

log_b(x+δ) − log_b(x) = log(1+δ/x)/log b
b > 0, b ≠ 1, x > 0, x+δ > 0

Why follow it

Iterating the identity gives a concrete way to study sensitivity through a deep tower while keeping the domain visible.

For positive real logarithms, subtract their arguments multiplicatively: log_b((x+δ)/x). Repeating this step is valid only while each intermediate logarithm remains defined.

The project applies it to the depth-three tower c^(c^(c^(2z))). Its recorded sweep at z = 1, 1.01 ≤ c ≤ 2.5, attained its smallest absolute remainder at the upper endpoint; this is a finite sweep result.

The exact transport equation and the sweep observation are separate pieces of evidence. A selected endpoint does not establish a universal preferred constant.
