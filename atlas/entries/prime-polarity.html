Does the arithmetic score survive its controls?

Prime/composite scoring becomes useful when the arithmetic transform, candidate mask and evaluation windows are kept explicit.

Prime Polarity conversations · November–December 2025 onward

Polarity index PI = 2·AUC − 1
Compare raw, difference, fractional-part and character transforms

Why follow it

It provides a reusable evaluation layer for the wheel, factorial valuations and proposed prime-boundary features.

Tie-aware AUC summarizes a score’s ordering ability. Odd-only and coprime masks help distinguish new information from the small-prime exclusions already built into the input.

Disjoint windows, fixed preprocessing and matched permutations keep training or threshold choices from leaking into evaluation.

A transform that already uses factorization or primality information must be described accordingly. No aggregate performance score was newly computed for this atlas entry.
