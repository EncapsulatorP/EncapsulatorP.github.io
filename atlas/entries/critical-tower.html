The ordinary tower that slows down near e

At the upper real tetration endpoint, the tower approaches its fixed point with a precise inverse-depth law.

The Pirate Chest · recovered edition · 21 November 2026

x₀=1,  x_(n+1)=exp(x_n/e)
x_n → e,  e−x_n ∼ 2e/n

Why follow it

It gives a sharp contrast to the uniform geometric convergence of the alternating tower.

For 0 < x < e, exp(x/e) lies above x and below e. Starting at 1, the iterates increase to the unique fixed point e.

Write δ_n=e−x_n. Taylor expansion gives δ_(n+1)=δ_n−δ_n²/(2e)+O(δ_n³), hence 1/δ_(n+1)−1/δ_n tends to 1/(2e). Averaging those increments yields nδ_n→2e.

This is the ordinary positive tower at base exp(1/e), with the stated starting value. The alternating tower is a different construction and has a different convergence mechanism.
