A bifurcation diagram wants a distribution

A parameter can lead to several orbit values. Modeling the conditional distribution preserves branches that a single mean prediction can hide.

Logistic and probability conversations · November–December 2025; February 2026

x_(n+1) = r x_n(1−x_n)
p(x | r) = Σ_j π_j(r) Beta(x; α_j(r), β_j(r))

Why follow it

It turns the old flat-regression problem into a clearly matched modeling task.

Squared-error regression targets a conditional mean. A bifurcation diagram is typically one-to-many, so a visually flat mean can coexist with structured conditional dynamics.

The recovered proposal used mixtures, quantiles or distributional models to preserve branches. Validation needs seed, burn-in, trajectory length and parameter-window separation.

A related probability-weight experiment is also reproducible: normalizing p_i^p_i for p = (0.1, 0.2, 0.7) gives approximately (0.345636, 0.315373, 0.338991), changing the ranking. A literal pointwise tower transform is not automatically a mode-sharpening operation.
