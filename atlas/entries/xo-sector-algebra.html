XO sectors: ten roots, five mirror pairs

The finite polynomial algebra behind one XO sector construction decomposes explicitly into roots and, at degree ten, into opposite pairs.

Recovered XO Atom sector conversation · 21 May 2026

A_n = ℂ[x]/(x^n−q^n),  q ≠ 0
A_n ≅ ℂ^n
n=10: roots {±q ζ₅^j : j=0,…,4}
Key formula

Ten roots. Five pairs. Each pair sums to zero.

Why follow it

It retains a concrete algebraic object from the broader exploratory vocabulary and gives the mirror cancellation an exact meaning.

For q ≠ 0, x^n−q^n has n distinct complex roots: its derivative n x^(n−1) is nonzero at every root. Evaluation at those roots gives the product decomposition by the Chinese remainder theorem.

At n = 10, the roots can be grouped as qζ₅^j and −qζ₅^j. Each pair has sum zero. q = 0 is a different, non-semisimple case and is excluded.

The result is finite polynomial algebra. A physical interpretation or a claimed cohomological invariant requires its own defined model and argument.

Next experiment or question

Write the evaluation and reconstruction maps explicitly, then test which proposed sector operations respect the algebra.
