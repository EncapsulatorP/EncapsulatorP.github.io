Goldbach fixes the centre; twins fix the gap

Centre and half-gap coordinates put additive prime pairs and twin primes in one exact arithmetic picture.

Hibernating Lemmas and the recovered prime-pair threads

m = (p + q)/2,  d = (q − p)/2
p = m − d,  q = m + d
pq = m² − d²

Why follow it

The same coordinates support a useful visual comparison without requiring an unproved implication between the two prime-pair questions.

For odd primes p ≤ q, m and d are integers. Goldbach representations of 2m lie at a fixed centre, while twin primes have d = 1.

The diagonal d = 0 corresponds to p = q and remains a valid Goldbach representation. The pair 2 + 2 is handled separately from the odd-prime picture.

The atlas exhibit enumerates unordered pairs exactly for its displayed finite range. Finding a point at every centre, or infinitely many points at d = 1, remains a separate question.
