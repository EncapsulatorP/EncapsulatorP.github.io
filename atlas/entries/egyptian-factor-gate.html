Egyptian fractions through a divisor gate

Fixing the first denominator turns a three-unit-fraction search into an exact factor-pair condition.

Remainder-compression conversations · 5–8 June 2026

a = nm,  A = km−1,  B = nm
k/n = 1/(nm) + 1/b + 1/c
⇔ (Ab−B)(Ac−B) = B²

Why follow it

The reciprocal geometry is useful in its own right: enumerate divisors, enforce congruences, and recover denominators exactly.

Take positive integers k, n, m with A > 0 and b, c > 0. Expanding the product gives A bc = B(b+c), equivalent to the remaining two unit fractions.

For positive factor pairs r·s = B² with r ≡ s ≡ −B modulo A, set b = (r+B)/A and c = (s+B)/A. Example: 4/5 = 1/5 + 1/2 + 1/10.

The restricted choice a = nm and any finite cap on m define the search scope. A failed search within that scope does not settle the general Erdős–Straus question.
