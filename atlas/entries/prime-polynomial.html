Reflection and translation in one prime polynomial

Goldbach counts and twin-prime counts are coefficients of finite products built from the same prime polynomial.

Goldbach p + q roadmap · November 2026

P_X(z) = Σ_{p ≤ X, p prime} z^p
G(N) = [z^N] P_N(z)²
T(X) = [z²] P_{X+2}(z) P_X(z⁻¹)

Why follow it

Reflection and translation give two clean algebraic interfaces for experiments and eventual formal proofs.

G(N) counts ordered pairs. If U(N) counts unordered pairs, then 2U(N) = G(N) + 1_{N/2 prime}; the extra term handles repeated-prime representations.

T(X) counts primes p ≤ X with p + 2 prime. The coefficient comes from (p + 2) − p = 2 in the Laurent product.

All products here are finite, so no analytic convergence assumption is needed. The companion checks all 149 even N from 4 through 300 against direct enumeration.
