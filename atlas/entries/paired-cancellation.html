Conjugate pairs cancel an antisymmetric kick

One of the paired model’s phase terms vanishes exactly when its two states are complex conjugates.

Paired Collatzscape model · 26 February 2026

w = conjugate(z) ⇒ Re C(w) = Re C(z)
φ_anti = (iγ/2)[f(Re C(z)) − f(Re C(w))] = 0

Why follow it

A visual asymmetry can be traced to a concrete condition: this particular direction term needs a mismatch in the pairing.

The cosine-Collatz extension commutes with conjugation because its coefficients are real. Equal real parts then give equal inputs to f, including the implemented tanh choice.

This statement concerns the displayed antisymmetric term. Other phase terms, parameters or numerical effects can still alter the paired evolution.

The exact cancellation supplies a useful control: compare conjugate initial states with a measured perturbation away from conjugacy.
