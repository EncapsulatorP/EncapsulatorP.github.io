CM points, tower fixed points and a controlled grammar

The surviving note compares nested combinations of modular quantities, prime-weighted series and a Lambert-W tower fixed point.

Partial conversation recovery · 16 February 2026

q a → q(a+cA) → q(a+cA+dM) → q(a+b|x*|+cA+dM)
A = Re((1−E₂)/E₄)
M = Re Σ_{n≥1} Λ(n)q^n/(1−q^n)

Why follow it

It preserves an unusual cross-family construction and gives its coefficient freedom an explicit role in the next comparison.

The original note compared CM points and reported that its apparent best fit changed when the allowed coefficient range changed. That makes the coefficient budget part of the experimental definition.

Here τ lies in the upper half-plane, q = exp(2πiτ) and |q| < 1. E₂ and E₄ are evaluated at τ, with E₄(τ) ≠ 0. The Lambert series is absolutely convergent in this domain.

The target denoted ρ, original script and complete search table were not recovered. The historical high-digit fit is therefore not promoted as a reproduced result.

A real tower subcase is precise: for q = exp(−a), a > 0, the positive fixed point of x = q^x is x = W₀(a)/a. Complex branches and the condition E₄ ≠ 0 must be specified before using the full grammar.
