REJECTED CLAIM

Does a flat tangent return every point?

This sentence is false. Its precise correction remains available below.

The correction

The local derivative identity is true. The global conclusion is false: the nonlinear fourth iterate is a degree-16 polynomial, not the identity on an interval.

Let f(x) = r x(1 − x), with r = 1 + √6. The two cycle slopes are −1 − √2 and −1 + √2. Their product is −1, so the derivative of the fourth iterate is 1 at either cycle point and the derivative of its displacement is zero there.

The leading coefficient of f is −r. Composing once more sends a leading coefficient a to −r a². Four compositions therefore have degree 16 and leading coefficient −r¹⁵, which is nonzero. Such a polynomial cannot agree with x on an interval.

An explicit starting point gives f⁴(1/2) − 1/2 = 1021(1 − √6)/65536, which is nonzero. Here f⁴ means fourfold composition, not the fourth power of a function value.

(f⁴ − id)′(x±) = 0 at the two-cycle points
degree(f⁴) = 16; leading coefficient = −r¹⁵
f⁴(1/2) − 1/2 = 1021(1 − √6)/65536 ≠ 0

What remains true

  • The paired linear-response matrix satisfies C² = −I and C⁴ = I.
