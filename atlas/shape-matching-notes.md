# Shapes and their mathematical companions

Edition: **21 November 2026**.

## Minimum discoveries on every map

Every body in both collections carries at least one Treasure, one Trap and one
Wildcard. `mapMinimumPicks` names the three guaranteed reading routes for each
body; the other six research stops remain available. The trio stays outside the
optional trail filters, and its map pins remain eligible for display regardless
of those filters. The ordinary perspective can hide a pin on the back of a body,
so the three text controls remain available even without the 3D renderer.

The trio establishes navigation coverage. The separate shape-connection note
states the strongest supported mathematical relationship. In particular, the
neck and Klein picks are shared research routes, with no claimed arithmetic
Ricci-flow or Klein-bottle correspondence. `verify_shape_catalog.cjs` checks the
minimum category coverage for all sixteen forms.

The paper workbench retains eight bodies in each of two collections and the same
nine numbered research stops. The new connections help a reader choose a route
without claiming that every body is produced by the mathematics beside it.

## Interaction

Selecting or hovering a body retains the user's choice. A compact connection
panel offers its relevant treasure where a connection is supportable. Choosing
one of those treasure buttons opens the logbook at that entry and clears topic
filters, so the requested destination remains visible.

When a reader selects a treasure from the numbered trail, an optional **Use
matching form** button offers its recommended body in the current collection.
There is no automatic body switch. If the current body already has a curated
connection, the logbook confirms it instead. The two tensegrity forms remain at
their previous enlarged sizes.

## Shared colors

`assets/atlas-theme.css` is an additive skin loaded after the room styles. The
entrance, navigation, room headers, and reading surfaces share warm paper,
dark ink, and teal controls. Mathematical instruments use a single dark teal
surface with their existing high-contrast plotted colors. Evidence categories,
the supplied source images, and data-color distinctions are retained.

The exact same paper token applies at phone widths; there is no mobile-only blue
page background after leaving the entrance. The standalone Recamán exhibit uses
the same public tokens.

## Validation scope

The existing shape-catalog and unfolding-engine checks exercise all sixteen
scenes and the unchanged nine destinations. Connection records must refer to
existing stop IDs and body keys. These checks concern geometry, navigation and
content integrity; they do not validate the research analogies as theorems.
