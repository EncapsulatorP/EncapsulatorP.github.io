A place in the research collection

The supplied Gemini study proposed delay-coordinate and Möbius views. Here, the delay view uses observed triples; the ribbon is labeled as an illustrative coordinate choice. Unvisited integers appear in a separate scalar rail so the picture does not invent missing triples.

The connected repository's saved mechanism report records a much larger finite experiment: 10,000,000 transitions and 103 selected catalogue targets, separating candidates never proposed from addition candidates bypassed by legal subtraction. This page computes its own smaller prefix locally; it does not rerun that report or prove that any value is permanently absent.
