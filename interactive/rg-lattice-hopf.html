Claim boundaryThe lattice is a synthetic 32 × 32 field built from the decimal digits of c in the chosen unit and a knot amplitude, then block-averaged. The layer names (Planck → thermodynamic) are Gemini’s illustrative labels, not a physical renormalization; “information lost” is Gemini’s heuristic. The Hopf panel is the exact normal form ṙ = μr − r³: a limit cycle of radius √μ when μ > 0 and a fixed point otherwise. Digits of c are a symbolic test alphabet, not a derivation of c.

Rules, limits & source notes

v(x, y) = clamp(0.42·hA + 0.22·hB + 0.36·wave, 0, 1); h = tower height of a digit ÷ 3; Layer k averages 2k × 2k blocks (k = 0…3).

  • Digit heights count prime factors with multiplicity (8 = 2³ → 3), capped at 2 unless the third layer is restored. hA reads digit (x + 3y), hB reads digit (5x + 7y), cycling through the integer part plus nine decimals of c in the chosen unit.
  • The wave term is sin(1.15t + 0.42x − 0.37y + a·cos(0.8t + 0.22·(7x ⊕ 11y))), with knot amplitude a = √((r − 1)/e) for r > 1 and 0.25 otherwise — the positive fixed point of Gemini’s Odd Mirror Knot.
  • Variance retained compares block-average variance with the raw field. “Information lost” is Gemini’s heuristic: log₂(1024 / cells) plus half the log-loss of variance. It is a readout of this construction, not a thermodynamic entropy.
  • Hopf panel: normalized normal form ṙ = μr − r³, so the stable cycle has R² = max(0, μ). With the digit wired in, μ = bias + 0.30 × (height − 2). The attractor dimension is that of the invariant set; the visible dimension and visible holes depend on the detector and floor. The live check confirms R² = max(0, μ).
  • Adapted: white instrument stage and the Atlas ramp (Gemini’s slate · sky · mint · gold became lavender · periwinkle · sapphire · amber); Next layer and Play/Pause added; animation pauses when hidden or when reduced motion is requested. Rules, ranges and defaults are Gemini’s.
