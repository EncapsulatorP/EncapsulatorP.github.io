Rules, limits & source notes

θ′ = 1; q′ = μ in both modes. Circle: reset q after each 2π turn. Helix: retain q and set height y = q.

  • Both modes now accumulate μ per unit time. The original wheel accumulated μ per complete turn while the helix accumulated μ per unit time, so its comparison mixed two rates.
  • The wheel’s burst marks its explicit reset. The helix’s rising trail depicts retention by definition; neither is evidence of a topological conservation or loss theorem.
  • The trail keeps at most 1,000 sampled positions. Pausing and orbiting the camera does not overwrite that history. The camera follows the helix vertically while preserving its viewing distance. The historical “Riemann Helix” title is recorded in the source inventory.
