Claim boundaryThis is a finite search for even totals from 20 through 200. Finding every pair in this range does not prove Goldbach’s conjecture.

Rules, limits & source notes

p = n − r, q = n + r; p + q = 2n. Both p and q must be prime.

  • The full pair table is recomputed for the selected center, independently of animation speed. Radius zero is included: a prime center gives the valid pair p = q = n.
  • The line and mirror arch preserve Gemini’s original center–radius idea. The scan now fits the full selected range on a phone. “Non-prime” includes 1, so it replaces the original “Composite” label.
  • Use Next radius for a discrete step, or Play animation to sweep the mirrors. The table contains every valid pair, even before the sweep reaches it.
