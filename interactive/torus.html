Bitemporal torus

A growing auxiliary variable accelerates a particle around a torus until a chosen reset increments a counter.

Claim boundaryThe reset and counter are defined animation rules in arbitrary units. This is not a physical model, demonstrated monodromy or construction of Riemann sheets. “Bitemporal” preserves the supplied concept’s name.

Rules, limits & source notes

x = (R + r cos v) cos u; y = r sin v; z = (R + r cos v) sin u. Here R = 10, r = 3.5.

  • Chosen update rules: m′ = growth, w′ = Gm, v′ = w, and u′ = speed. A finite time step approximates these rules. When v reaches π, the counter increments and m,w reset to zero; v is set to −π/2.
  • At v = π the particle reaches the inner rim, whose distance from the symmetry axis is R − r = 6.5. It does not reach the center of the torus hole. The correction replaces the original “hole center” wording.
  • The torus, moving particle and event colors are preserved. Physical units such as amu and m/s were removed. Reset restores the initial color and state. Motion starts paused when the device requests reduced motion.
